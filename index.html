HOW TO OPERATE A HUMAN(ISH)

THE ART OF NOT FALLING

Every body part is controlled separately. In the future, five friends will operate this one puppet with their phones. For now, try all controls in this single-player lab.

  1. Walk: Hold A to lift the left foot, then release to plant it and push forward. Alternate with D.
  2. Wave your arms: Press J / K to swing arms, add momentum and help recover.
  3. Save your balance: Press ← / → to lean. SPACE braces your core for 0.65 seconds, with a cooldown.
  4. Avoid hazards: Time steps or brace when you reach the cones.
  5. Recover: Even if you fall, Bongo gets back up in about two seconds. No elimination!

Tap the large colored buttons if you're using a phone or tablet. For a hands-free demonstration, use WATCH AI DEMO.
