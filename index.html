HOW TO OPERATE A HUMAN(ISH)

THE ART OF NOT FALLING

Every body part is controlled separately. In the future, five friends will operate this one puppet with their phones. For now, try all controls in this single-player lab.

  1. Left step: Hold A + K + RIGHT ARROW for about 0.35 seconds, then release A while holding K and →.
  2. Right step: Hold D + J + LEFT ARROW for about 0.35 seconds, then release D while holding J and ←.
  3. Arms: The opposite arm must counter-swing. In Cake Carry mode, BOTH arms must hold the tray throughout.
  4. Torso: Shift weight toward the planted foot. Wrong direction causes a slip. SPACE braces temporarily but does NOT replace coordination.
  5. Avoid hazards: Time a correctly coordinated lifted foot over the cones, or brace. In Cake Carry, tilting or releasing a hand loses layers.
  6. Recover: Even if you fall, Bongo gets back up in about two seconds. No elimination!

Tap the large colored buttons if you're using a phone or tablet. For a hands-free demonstration, use WATCH TEAMWORK DEMO.
